    Your connection

    Check the customer and server before you act.

    Sign in through Realinsight, confirm the customer and requested permissions, then ask for your connection context. The server URL identifies the deployment. Production is the default; approved QA testers use a separate QA connection.

    A hosted installation has a fixed endpoint. To change customers, disconnect and reconnect through your agent app and choose the intended customer during sign-in. A different browser login does not retarget an existing connection. Local toolkit users can authorize and switch separate saved profiles.

    To revoke access, use the hosted disconnect tool after approval or the Realinsight Connection Manager. Your host may also require removing its saved connection. Data already returned to your conversation is not removed by disconnecting.

    Tool availability depends on your deployment, permissions, and scopes. The connector does not send email, search the public web, or administer user access. Read the authentication guide and security guidance, or visit Realinsight technical support.
